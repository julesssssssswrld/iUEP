About iUEP

iUEP is the University of Eastern Philippines' official student-driven portal. It was born from a desire to bridge the gap between physical campus life and digital convenience. Our mission is to put the university experience in the pockets of every UEPian.

Previously, essential tasks like checking grades or applying for a Student ID often meant physically visiting department buildings. We have transformed that process by creating a cloud-native platform that centralizes the information you need most.

Campus News Hub

iUEP aggregates live updates from official university sources, including the USC, UPMAO, The Pillar, and various college councils. We use automated scraping to ensure you stay informed without needing to search across multiple platforms.

Digital ID System

Our modernized identification workflow streamlines the student ID application and production process. It provides a secure, digital-first approach to campus identity management, integrated with real-time status notifications.

Academic Monitoring

Access your grades and academic records securely from anywhere. We believe that your academic information should be accessible at your fingertips, reducing the need for physical visits to departmental offices.
